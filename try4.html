UGFXLAB ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of visitors, students, customers, and members of our trading education community.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, register for our programs, purchase mentorship services, participate in our community, or communicate with us.

By accessing or using our website and services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, you can contact us at:

General Support: support@ugfxlab.com

Business & Partnerships: partneships@ugfxlab.com

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through:

  • Our official website.

  • Email and other communication channels.

03. Information We Collect

We may collect different categories of personal information depending on how you interact with UGFXLAB.

A. Personal and Contact Information

This may include:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Country and city of residence.

When you register for a program or mentorship, we may collect:

When you purchase a program, we may receive information such as:

If you participate in our community or contact us, we may collect:

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

Where applicable, we may collect information about your interaction with our marketing communications, including email opens, clicks, newsletter subscriptions, and interactions with our social media content.
