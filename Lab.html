THE LAB | Mentorship Journey
THE LAB MENTORSHIP

Six months.
A different trader.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Market Foundations

Build a solid understanding of the financial markets, how price moves, and what drives market behaviour. Learn the language of trading before learning how to trade.

02 / TECHNICAL ANALYSIS

Technical Analysis

Learn to read price action, identify market structure, and understand key levels. Develop the ability to interpret charts with clarity instead of relying on guesswork or random indicators.

03 / THE METHODOLOGY

Our Trading Methodology

Put the pieces together through a structured trading framework. Understand setups, confluences, entry models, and the conditions that make a trade worth taking — and when to stay out.

04 / RISK & EXECUTION

Risk Management & Execution

Learn to manage risk before thinking about returns. Refine position sizing, stop-loss placement, trade execution, and decision-making so your strategy can be applied with discipline.

05 / PSYCHOLOGY & CONSISTENCY

Psychology & Consistency

Develop the mindset to follow your plan through wins, losses, and uncertainty. Review your performance, recognise behavioural patterns, and build repeatable habits for long-term improvement.
